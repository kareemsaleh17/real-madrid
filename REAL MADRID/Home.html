Welcome to the world of Real Madrid

Discover the legacy of the most successful football club in history. Real Madrid Club de Fútbol, commonly known as Real Madrid, is one of the most successful and widely supported professional football clubs in the world. Based in Madrid, Spain, the club is a titan of the sport, holding records for both domestic and international titles.

UEFA Champions League

A record-extending 15th title was added in May 2024. They are the only club to win three consecutive titles in the modern era (2016–2018).

La Liga

They hold the record for the most Spanish league titles. One of their most famous wins was the 2011/12 "League of Records" under José Mourinho, where they reached 100 points and 121 goals.

FIFA Club World Cup / Intercontinental Cup

They hold the record for the most world club titles, including their most recent Intercontinental Cup victory in late 2024.

Copa del Rey

Real Madrid have won the Copa del Rey around 20 times.Their first title came in 1905, making them one of the earliest winners.Their most recent victory in the Spanish Cup came in the 2022/23 season.
